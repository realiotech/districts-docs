The development of Districts is structured in phases, each focusing on different aspects of the platform's growth and functionality.

Phase 1 - LandRush:

We kicked off with the LandRush event on April 15th, 2025, where virtual parcels in New York were available to claim. We will gradually unveil Districts. This phase is crucial for those looking to secure their spot in the burgeoning virtual world of Districts.<br />
