The development of Districts is structured in phases, each focusing on different aspects of the platform's growth and functionality.

Phase 2 - Build:

Users will have the opportunity to shape their own land. This is where creativity meets reality, as users bring their unique visions to life within the virtual landscape. This phase empowers users to construct, design, and personalize their virtual spaces with different design tools.
