title: "How Districts innovates virtual land ownership?"
sidebar_label: "How Districts innovates virtual land ownership?"
---

Districts redefines virtual land ownership by integrating blockchain technology with a decentralized, community-driven economy. Built on the [Realio Network](https://realio.network/), Districts leverages smart contracts and digital assets to facilitate land ownership, governance, and platform interactions while contributing to the broader network’s activity and utility.

At the heart of Districts is the [Land Bank](https://districts.xyz/files/Districts_Token_Economy_Whitepaper.pdf), a decentralized mechanism that ensures each parcel of land is backed by a $RIO (Realio Network Token) deposit. This structure anchors participation in the ecosystem, allowing users to return land if desired while reinforcing liquidity and engagement across both Districts and the Realio Network. This interconnected model incentivizes long-term participation—aligning network growth with user activity.

By leveraging the modular and scalable nature of the Realio Network, Districts creates an open, evolving platform where users actively shape its future. Governance through $DSTRX, the governance token of Districts, empowers participants to vote on expansions, platform changes, and new features, ensuring that Districts remains dynamic and user-driven.

As participation grows, so does the utility of the ecosystem, reinforcing Districts as a platform where virtual land ownership, collaboration, and governance merge to create a truly sustainable and decentralized digital world.
