Yes — the LandRush is open to anyone who wants to explore and claim land.

Here's what you need to claim land:

* 5,000 $RIO on the Realio Network (plus gas fees) — This is your key to claiming land.
* EVM-Compatible Web3 Wallet (like MetaMask) — You’ll use this wallet to store and connect your Native $RIO, as well as receive and manage the Land Pixels and $DSTRX you receive when claiming land in Districts.

For a full step-by-step guide on participating in the Districts LandRush, check out this [guide](https://www.districts.xyz/landrush). To learn how to navigate various features in Districts, check out our [video library.](../../branding-guidelines/districts-brand-kit/video-library.md)
