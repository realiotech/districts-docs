# Who is Districts for?

Districts is for anyone looking to participate in the next evolution of digital ownership—from blockchain enthusiasts to creative professionals, real estate visionaries, and community builders.

Unlike traditional virtual spaces, Districts is a decentralized, user-driven platform where land ownership is backed by the Realio Network’s economic infrastructure, ensuring real utility beyond speculation. Whether you’re looking to claim and develop land, contribute to decentralized governance, or explore new opportunities in digital economies, Districts provides an open framework for participation.
