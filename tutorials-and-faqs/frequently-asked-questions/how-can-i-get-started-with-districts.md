# How can I get started with Districts?

To claim land, you'll need:

* 5,000 $RIO on the Realio Network (plus gas fees)
* EVM-Compatible Web3 Wallet (like MetaMask) — You’ll use this wallet to store and connect your Native $RIO, as well as receive and manage the Land Pixels and $DSTRX you receive when claiming land in Districts.

Check out this guide for a full step-by-step guide on participating in the Districts LandRush. To learn how to navigate various features in Districts, check out our [video library](../../branding-guidelines/districts-brand-kit/video-library.md).
